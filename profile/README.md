**Hivematic** is a SaaS platform to build, run and govern AI agents inside your company. Agents handle customer conversations, orchestrate multi-step workflows and execute business processes across the tools you already use — with humans in the loop where it matters.

- 🤖 **Agents** with built-in tools, memory and escalation to people
- 🔀 **Workflows** that chain agents, approvals and integrations
- 🔌 **Integrations** with Slack, WhatsApp, Freshdesk, SendGrid and more
- 🧠 **Model-agnostic**: works with all the major LLM providers
- 🇪🇺 **European by design**: GDPR and EU AI Act compliance, human oversight and audit trails
